Vane is a simple, clear weather app. Open it and see the weather where you are, hour by hour, with no account to create and no ads.

WEATHER WHERE YOU ARE
• Today's temperature, the high and low, and a short summary of the conditions
• An hourly forecast for the rest of the day
• Today's details at a glance: UV index, precipitation, sunrise and sunset, pressure, wind speed and wind direction
• Press and hold any detail for a plain-English explanation of what it means
• Approximate location is enough

RAIN AND SNOW ALERTS
Turn on alerts in Settings and Vane sends you a notification about an hour before rain, snow or a thunderstorm starts where you last opened the app. You get one alert per spell of weather, not a stream of them.

YOUR FAVOURITE PLACES
Search for any town or city and save it. The Favourites tab shows the weather for every saved place, and you can tap one for its full forecast.

A HOME SCREEN WIDGET
Add the Vane widget to see the current weather, the next few hours and your favourites without opening the app. It keeps showing the last forecast when you're offline.

LIGHT OR DARK
Follow your phone's theme, or choose light or dark in Settings.
